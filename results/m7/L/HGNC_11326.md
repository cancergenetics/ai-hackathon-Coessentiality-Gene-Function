**Summary.** SSR4 encodes the delta subunit of the translocon-associated protein (TRAP) complex, an ER membrane heterotetramer (SSR1-4) that supports protein translocation and N-linked glycosylation. Loss-of-function variants cause the X-linked congenital disorder of glycosylation SSR4-CDG, and knockout cells show glycosylation defects during ER stress. Function is thus established by focused experimental and clinical work, although molecular detail specific to the delta subunit remains limited.
