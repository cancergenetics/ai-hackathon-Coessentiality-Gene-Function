C16orf87 (renamed MHAP1 or HDIP in 2025-2026 reports) encodes a small protein that is a subunit of the MIER1-HDAC1/2 corepressor complex, as shown by interaction proteomics, cross-linking MS structural modelling, and knockout in human cells and zebrafish. Loss alters chromatin accessibility, cell migration and zebrafish embryonic development. The evidence is very recent and from a few focused studies, with UniProt still lacking annotation; some of it is preprint-derived.
