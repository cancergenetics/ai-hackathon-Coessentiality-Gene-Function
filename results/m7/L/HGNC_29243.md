Summary: RBM27 is a nuclear-speckle/cytospeckle RRM protein that, together with RBM26, ZC3H3 and PABPN1, is required for PAXT-mediated decay of nuclear polyadenylated RNA and PROMPT ncRNAs, and it assists PABPN1-dependent splicing of last introns with weak 3' splice sites. Its C. elegans ortholog protects neurons by regulating a mitoribosomal assembly factor. Cancer/epilepsy expression studies are correlative.
