NDUFA11 is a supernumerary membrane subunit of mitochondrial complex I, located at the complex I/III interface and important for supercomplex (respirasome) stability. A splice-site mutation causes human complex I deficiency (MC1DN14), and knockdown experiments and the C. elegans orthologue support a role in complex I assembly and respirasome maintenance, and a 2026 preprint cites DepMap CRISPR data as showing pan-cancer essentiality.
