COMMD10 is a conserved subunit of the ten-member COMM-domain ring in the CCC complex, which together with CCDC22/CCDC93 and Retriever forms the Commander complex driving endosomal recycling of transmembrane cargo. Experimental studies in cells and mice link it to NF-kB inhibition, ENaC trafficking, macrophage phagolysosomal maturation, tissue-resident macrophage survival and embryonic neural crest development. The cellular function is established by focused work, though its individual mechanistic contribution within the ring is less defined.
