**Summary.** YLPM1 (ZAP113) is a large nuclear protein with YLP repeat motifs that localises to nuclear speckles and, by similarity, interacts with PPP1CA, NCOA5 and RNA-binding factors and represses TERT. The retrieved literature is mainly population genetics (positive selection in clonal hematopoiesis, obesity and psychiatric associations) and one tumour study linking inactivation to GIST growth. No focused mechanistic study of the protein itself was found.
