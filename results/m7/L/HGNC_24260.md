MED31 is a small subunit of the middle module of the Mediator coactivator complex that bridges gene-specific regulators and RNA polymerase II. In human cells MED31 is reported to promote recycling of phosphorylated MED1 and Pol II to boost mRNA output and its knockdown reduces mesenchymal stem cell self-renewal, while mouse hypomorphic alleles cause reduced fetal growth and Drosophila Med31 is needed for host defence. Most other literature is ortholog work in yeast, plants and protists or association studies (methylation, GWAS).
