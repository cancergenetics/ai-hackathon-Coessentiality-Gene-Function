ELP6 (C3orf75) is the sixth subunit of the human Elongator complex, forming the ELP456 sub-complex with ELP4 and ELP5, which catalyses wobble-uridine tRNA modifications (mcm5U, mcm5s2U, ncm5U) and regulates mRNA decoding efficiency. It was identified as an Elongator subunit in 2012 when its depletion in melanoma cells impaired tumorigenicity and migration, and ELP6 variants have been found in patients with developmental delay, epilepsy and intellectual disability with reduced Elongator activity. Further work covers plant ortholog interactions, yeast repression memory and cancer prognostic associations.
