ELP5 (DERP6) is a core subunit of the six-subunit Elongator complex, where together with ELP4 and ELP6 it forms the ELP456 subcomplex and links ELP3 to ELP4 for complex integrity. Elongator catalyses wobble uridine (mcm5/ncm5U34) tRNA modification, and ELP5 loss abolishes this modification and affects p53/hnRNPQ translation, gemcitabine response and melanoma cell migration. Function is established by focused experimental work including structural studies in yeast.
