**Summary.** YPEL5 is a small conserved yippee-family protein that is a stoichiometric subunit of the CTLH/GID multisubunit RING E3 ubiquitin ligase, where it binds WDR26 and modulates substrate recognition. It was originally linked to cell-cycle progression through RanBPM/RanBP10 and recent structural work shows it can inhibit NMNAT1 ubiquitylation. Function is therefore established by focused biochemical and structural studies.
