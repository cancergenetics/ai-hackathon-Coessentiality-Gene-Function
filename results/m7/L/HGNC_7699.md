NDUFB4 (B15) is a single-pass membrane accessory subunit of mitochondrial complex I whose structure places it at the interface with complex III in the I1III2IV1 respirasome. A 2024 mutational study shows that specific NDUFB4 residues are required for respirasome assembly and respiratory flux, in addition to systematic knockout data showing the accessory subunits are needed for complex assembly. Function is established by focused experimental work, though the role is as a complex I accessory/supercomplex subunit rather than a catalytic one.
