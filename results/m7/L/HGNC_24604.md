COA4 (CHCHD8) is a twin-CX9C mitochondrial intermembrane-space protein and putative cytochrome c oxidase (COX) assembly factor; yeast Coa4 was placed in the copper-delivery route to Cox1 via Cox11, and yeast cells lacking it have reduced COX activity. In human, a 2025 study identifies COA4 as a conserved COX regulator that is overexpressed in KRAS-mutant tumours and promotes metastasis through mitochondrial metabolism and CDC42 activation. Remaining human literature is associative (biomarker, autoantibody and expression studies).
