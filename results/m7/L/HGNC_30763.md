NR2C2AP (TRA16) is a small nuclear protein first isolated as a binding partner of the orphan nuclear receptor TR4 (NR2C2), where reporter, two-hybrid, co-IP and EMSA assays indicate it selectively represses TR4-mediated transactivation. A later study reports that it also interacts with TR2 and ERbeta and promotes proliferation, and it is overexpressed in non-small cell lung cancer. The evidence is from one research group using overexpression and reporter systems with no in vivo or loss-of-function mechanism, so the class is partial (borderline N1/N2).
