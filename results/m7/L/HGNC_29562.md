MARF1 (LKAP/limkain b1) is an RNA-binding endoribonuclease with an N-terminal NYN domain and C-terminal LOTUS domains, and mouse knockouts show it is essential for oocyte meiosis and retrotransposon silencing. In human somatic cells it localises to P-bodies, binds EDC4/Ge-1 and the DCP1:DCP2 decapping complex and degrades target mRNAs. Function is well established by focused biochemical, structural and genetic work.
