C5orf34 is an uncharacterised mammalian-conserved protein with no UniProt functional annotation. All published work is cancer-focused: it is upregulated in several tumours, siRNA knockdown reduces lung adenocarcinoma proliferation and migration, and these studies suggest involvement of MAPK signalling and the cell cycle. No biochemical function, interaction partner or localisation has been established, so the evidence is phenotypic and indirect.
