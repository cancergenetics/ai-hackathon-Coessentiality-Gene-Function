NDUFB2 (AGGG) is a supernumerary accessory subunit of mitochondrial respiratory complex I in the membrane arm, identified as a complex I component by immunopurification proteomics. Its role comes from systematic knockout and proteomics of all accessory subunits and from structural work on the complex; no NDUFB2-specific focused study or confirmed human disease variant was found. Remaining literature is bioinformatic cancer or disease association lists.
