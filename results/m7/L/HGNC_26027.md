COMMD4 is a COMM-domain protein in the CCC subcomplex of the Commander complex and, with COMMD9, is among the genes mutated in Ritscher-Schinzel syndrome, a disorder recast as an endosomal recyclinopathy. Independently of Commander, nuclear COMMD4 binds histone H2B and protects it from RNF20/RNF40 monoubiquitination to support timely DNA double-strand-break repair, and a H2B-mimicking peptide disrupts this interaction in lung cancer cells. COMMD4 also promotes melanoma PI3K-AKT signalling, neuronal ferroptosis in ALS models, and was identified as a cuproptosis sensor in a preprint.
