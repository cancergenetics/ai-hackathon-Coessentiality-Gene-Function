DDX19A is a cytoplasmic/nucleoplasmic DEAD-box ATPase of the DDX19/Dbp5 subfamily that associates with the nuclear pore complex and, together with paralog DDX19B, remodels mRNPs to terminate mRNA export. In leukaemia cells DDX19A and DDX19B jointly regulate MCL1 mRNA nuclear export and selinexor sensitivity, and DDX19A has further reported roles in R-loop homeostasis with LSD1, and in NLRP3 inflammasome activation (porcine model). Several cancer studies (gastric, cervical) link DDX19A overexpression to proliferation and metastasis via PI3K/AKT and NOX1-ROS.
