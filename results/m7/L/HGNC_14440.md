**Summary.** FAM117B is a poorly characterised protein; recent studies in gastric, colorectal and liver cancer cells report that it binds KEAP1 and competes with NRF2, and also interacts with DYRK1A to promote PLK2 phosphorylation. Genetic studies repeatedly nominate the 2q33 locus for stroke, small-vessel disease and sarcoidosis. The mechanistic work is recent and cancer-cell based.
