**Summary.** MTRES1 (C6orf203) is a small matrix-localised mitochondrial protein with an S4-like RNA-binding domain. It binds structured RNA, associates with the mitoribosomal large subunit, supports mitochondrial translation and OXPHOS, and prevents loss of mitochondrial transcripts under stress. With MTRFR it also ejects peptidyl-tRNA from stalled mitoribosomes as part of mitoribosome-associated quality control.
