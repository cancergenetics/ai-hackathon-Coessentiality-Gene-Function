PGS1 encodes the mitochondrial phosphatidylglycerophosphate synthase catalysing the committed step of phosphatidylglycerol and cardiolipin synthesis, established by yeast and hamster genetics and biochemistry. Human PGS1 oligomerises and associates with CLS1, PTPMT1 and cardiolipin-binding membrane proteins, and its depletion lowers cardiolipin and alters mitochondrial dynamics in OPA1-deficient fibroblasts. Function is established by focused experimental studies.
