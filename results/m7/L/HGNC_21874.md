FBXL18 is an F-box protein that assembles into SCF (SKP1-CUL1) E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes. Multiple recent studies in infection and cancer report substrate-level ubiquitination, including K11-ubiquitination of BST2 and ubiquitination of PTEN, RPS15A and DUSP16, with pro-oncogenic signalling consequences. Evidence is mostly disease-context based and the physiological substrate set is not yet established.
