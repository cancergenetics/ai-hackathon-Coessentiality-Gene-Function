CNOT11 (formerly C2orf29) is a vertebrate-specific non-catalytic subunit of the CCR4-NOT deadenylase that docks with CNOT10 onto the N-terminal region of the CNOT1 scaffold and forms a structural platform with a conserved antenna-like domain for partner proteins such as GGNBP2 and SCAPER. It is needed for CCR4-NOT-dependent tubulin mRNA autoregulation and for sensing of unedited cellular dsRNA. Global Cnot11 knockout mice are not recovered and liver-specific deletion delays hepatocyte maturation, while cancer-cell depletion links it to autophagy and IL-6/JAK/STAT signalling.
