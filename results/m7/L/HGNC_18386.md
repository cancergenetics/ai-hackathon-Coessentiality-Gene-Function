**Summary.** SFTA2 (SP-G) encodes a small secreted protein found in alveolar type II cells and bronchiolar epithelium and named a putative surfactant protein because it lacks sequence similarity to classical SP-A to SP-D. Follow-up work detects it in cerebrospinal fluid, tear film and laryngeal epithelium and suggests surface activity from modelling, while cancer papers describe it as a prognostic marker. No mechanism of action has been established in the retrieved work apart from a single recent colorectal cancer study.
