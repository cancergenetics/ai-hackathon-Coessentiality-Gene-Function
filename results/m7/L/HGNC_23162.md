ALG10 is an ER-membrane alpha-1,2-glucosyltransferase that completes the lipid-linked oligosaccharide precursor for N-glycosylation; its enzymatic role is annotated mainly by homology to the paralog ALG10B and yeast Alg10. Direct experimental work on the human protein is limited to an IBD-fibrosis paper and preprints using Drosophila and GWAS data, and no congenital disorder of glycosylation has been firmly assigned. Gene-specific biochemistry of human ALG10 was not retrieved.
