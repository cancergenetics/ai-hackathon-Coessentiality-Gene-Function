TMEM208 (hSND2) is an ER membrane protein of the signal-independent (SND) targeting pathway; it engages the substrate-binding domain of signal recognition particle (SRP) to accelerate cargo release and hand over nascent proteins to translocation machinery, and it contributes to ER targeting of GPI-anchored proteins with weak signals. Loss of the Drosophila ortholog causes lethality with polarity and developmental defects. Earlier annotations (negative regulator of ER-stress autophagy, HIF-1alpha target with a WH2 motif) and cancer prognostic studies are less mechanistic.
