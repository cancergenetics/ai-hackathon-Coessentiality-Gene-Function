Summary: CCDC174 is a ubiquitous nuclear protein that associates with the exon junction complex component EIF4A3 and with the PRP19/CDC5L splicing complex, and binds U1 snRNA to promote selection of weak 5' splice sites. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause an infantile hypotonia/psychomotor retardation syndrome (IHPMR) and female infertility through oocyte alternative-splicing defects. Its exact molecular role within the spliceosome/EJC remains to be defined.
