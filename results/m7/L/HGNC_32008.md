TMEM120B is a multi-pass transmembrane protein of the nuclear inner membrane, paralogous to TMEM120A (TACAN), that is enriched in adipose tissue and required for efficient adipocyte differentiation in cell-culture knockdown experiments. A recent cancer study shows it binds and stabilises MYH9 to promote integrin/FAK-TAZ-mTOR signalling, and biophysical work shows it can bind CoA/ATP in bilayers. The physiological molecular function remains unresolved, so the class is partial (borderline N1/N2).
