**Summary.** ZNF318 is a nuclear zinc-finger protein annotated in UniProt (by similarity to the mouse ortholog) as a coregulator of androgen receptor signalling. Mouse Zfp318 is a transcriptional regulator required for IgD expression in mature B cells, for memory B cell mitochondrial programs, and for normal spermatogenesis. Human-specific evidence is mostly genetic (clonal hematopoiesis, infertility variants), so the class rests on ortholog experiments.
