TM4SF19 is an L6-family tetraspanin that localises to lysosomal membranes and negatively regulates V-ATPase by binding V0 subunits (ATP6V0B/ATP6V0D2), thereby reducing lysosomal acidification in lipid-associated macrophages. It is required for osteoclast multinucleation and bone loss in mice, and its extracellular-loop decoy LEL-Fc attenuates arthritis and psoriasis models. It also modulates YAP transcription via the GABP complex in head and neck cancer under oxidative stress.
