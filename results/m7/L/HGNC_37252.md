ARHGEF33 is a predicted Rho guanine nucleotide exchange factor. The only experimental study, in zebra finch, shows that the avian orthologue has GEF activity towards RhoA, is enriched in Muller glia of the developing parafovea and, when overexpressed, induces cell contraction and inhibits neurite extension. Human data are limited to proteomic/expression surveys and a mutation mention, so function is partially characterised and based on an ortholog.
