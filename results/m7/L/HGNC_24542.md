DPCD (deleted in primary ciliary dyskinesia) is a ciliary-associated protein whose expression rises during ciliated-cell differentiation and whose loss in mice causes hydrocephalus, situs inversus and male infertility from motile-cilia defects, with loss of inner dynein arms. It was identified as an adaptor of the R2TP (RUVBL1/RUVBL2) chaperone complex, and the R2TP-DPCD module regulates ciliogenesis initiation by controlling Akt phosphorylation. Its structure is a heart-shaped monomer of two domains that binds RUVBL1/RUVBL2.
