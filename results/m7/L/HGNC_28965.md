Summary: RBM34 is a nucleolar RRM-containing RNA-binding protein with no curated molecular function in UniProt. Experimental work is limited to cancer studies in which RBM34 knockout reduces proliferation/migration and FGFR2/PI3K-AKT signalling in HCC, plus a suggested CMSS1/DDX5 interaction in NSCLC. Its RNA targets and role in nucleolar RNA processing are not established.
