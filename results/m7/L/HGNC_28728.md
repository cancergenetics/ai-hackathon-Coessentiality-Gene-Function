Summary: MARCHF3 (MARCH3, RNF173) is an endosome/plasma-membrane-associated RING-CH E3 ubiquitin ligase related to MARCH2. Focused studies show it ubiquitinates and degrades cytokine receptors (IL-1RI, IL-6R, IL-5Ra, IL-3Ra) and FcgRIIb, restraining inflammatory signalling, and in HCC it targets GRB2, GPX4 and PARP1. It also influences endothelial barrier function.
