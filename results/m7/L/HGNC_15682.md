**Summary.** PHC3 is the human homologue of Drosophila polyhomeotic and a subunit of canonical PRC1-like Polycomb complexes (hPRC-H) that maintain gene repression and H2A monoubiquitination. It also associates with E2F6 during G0, its SAM domain forms polymers regulated by its linker, and its expression is reduced in osteosarcoma. Most subsequent literature treats PHC3 as a PRC1 component rather than studying it individually.
