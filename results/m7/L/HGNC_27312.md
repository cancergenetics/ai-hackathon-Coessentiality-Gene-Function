Summary: SAMD14 is a sterile-alpha-motif protein whose best-characterised role, from mouse erythroid studies, is promoting SCF/Kit signalling, erythroid progenitor survival and regeneration under anemic stress. Its SAM domain is required for this and it interacts with F-actin capping protein and binds PI3P to balance autophagy. In humans it is also an autoantigen in primary CNS lymphoma and is hypermethylated in some cancers; UniProt gives no functional curation.
