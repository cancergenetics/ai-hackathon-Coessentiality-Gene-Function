SMYD4 is a SET/MYND-domain protein-lysine methyltransferase that, in human cancer cells, monomethylates PRMT5 and MYH9 and has been implicated in cardiac development through HDAC1 interaction in model organisms. Reports conflict on whether it behaves as an oncogene (HCC) or tumour suppressor (breast). Substrate and phenotype data are recent and mostly cancer-cell based.
