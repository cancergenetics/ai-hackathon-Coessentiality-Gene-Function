**Summary.** H2AJ encodes H2A.J, a poorly studied histone H2A variant found only in mammals. It accumulates in senescent cells and ageing tissues, marks persistent DNA damage foci, alters higher-order chromatin organisation and promotes the senescence-associated secretory phenotype, shown with knockout mice and knockdown cells. Further reports in glioma, liver fibrosis and colorectal cancer are expression/knockdown studies.
