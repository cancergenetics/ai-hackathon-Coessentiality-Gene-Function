MID1IP1 (MIG12, Spot 14-related protein) is a small lipogenic regulator that promotes polymerisation and activation of acetyl-CoA carboxylase and is an LXR target gene in liver cells. Human cell studies show that depleting it reduces lipogenic gene expression, and several cancer papers show it promotes proliferation, stemness and c-Myc stability. Mechanistic annotation in UniProt is largely inferred from mouse.
