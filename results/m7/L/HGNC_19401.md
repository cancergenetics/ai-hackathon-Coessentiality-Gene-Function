PGBD4 is a domesticated piggyBac-derived gene with no gene-specific mechanistic study in the retrieved literature. Family-level work shows that human PGBDs lack transposition activity on piggyBac-like elements and have stress- and cell-type-dependent expression, but the function of PGBD4 itself is unknown. It otherwise appears only in variant-burden or expression gene lists.
