NHLRC2 is a ubiquitous cytosolic NHL-repeat protein of unknown biochemical function that is essential for mammalian development, since Nhlrc2-null mouse embryos arrest at gastrulation. Biallelic variants (recurrently p.Asp148Tyr) cause FINCA syndrome (fibrosis, neurodegeneration, cerebral angiomatosis), and a FINCA mouse model shows altered hnRNP C2 in developing neurons plus behavioural and immune changes; a CRISPR screen also ranks it among genes required for erythroid differentiation. Even recent clinical papers state that its specific physiological or enzymatic role remains unknown.
