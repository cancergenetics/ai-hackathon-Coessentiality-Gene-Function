Summary: CTU2 is the scaffold-like subunit of the cytosolic thiouridylase CTU1/CTU2, which, with URM1 and the sulfur-relay machinery, installs the 2-thio group on wobble uridine (U34) of tRNA-Lys, -Glu and -Gln. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause DREAM-PL syndrome with defective tRNA thiolation, and loss causes codon-specific translational/proteostasis defects. Tumours (melanoma, HCC) exploit U34-modification enzymes for codon-biased translation.
