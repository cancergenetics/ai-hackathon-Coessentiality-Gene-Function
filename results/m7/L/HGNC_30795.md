TSPAN11 is a four-transmembrane tetraspanin family member. The only focused experimental study shows that Tspan11 silencing in mouse osteoblasts disrupts focal adhesion maturation, osteoblast alignment and orthogonal bone matrix formation; Drosophila Tsp74F mutants show integrin mislocalisation. Human data are limited to association mentions (Kallmann syndrome candidate lists, CD151 modifier speculation) and bioinformatic expression studies, so function is only partially characterised.
