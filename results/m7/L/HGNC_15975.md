**Summary.** SASH3 (SLy1) is a lymphocyte-restricted adaptor protein with SAM and SH3 domains thought to organise signalling complexes downstream of antigen receptors. Hemizygous loss-of-function variants cause an X-linked combined immunodeficiency with CD4 lymphopenia, impaired T-cell proliferation and increased apoptosis, rescued by cDNA transfer, recapitulating Sly1-deficient mouse phenotypes. Its precise biochemical mechanism remains incompletely defined.
