Summary: DNHD1 is a dynein-heavy-chain-family protein localised along the sperm flagellum and required for assembly of the flagellar axoneme, including the central pair and mitochondrial sheath. Bi-allelic loss-of-function variants cause asthenoteratozoospermia/MMAF (SPGF65) in humans and Dnhd1 knockout mice are infertile. Peripheral reports link it to laterality in zebrafish and to cancer expression, but its molecular mechanism is not defined.
