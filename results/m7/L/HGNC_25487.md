MED9 is a small subunit of the middle module of the Mediator coactivator complex, forming a Med4/Med9 heterodimer within the middle-module tetramer. Mechanistic work (two functional domains, roles in activation and repression) comes from yeast, with plant orthologs studied in stress and defence responses. In humans only complex membership by UniProt annotation and associative data (a short MED9 isoform in dilated cardiomyopathy, plasma-protein longevity predictors) exist.
