**Summary.** WDR7 (rabconnectin-3 beta) is a WD-repeat protein that, with DMXL1/DMXL2 and ROGDI, forms the mammalian RAVE-like complex that promotes assembly of the proton-pumping V-ATPase on endolysosomal and secretory vesicles. Knockdown and CRISPR-screen studies link it to vesicle acidification and viral entry, and structural/biochemical work in 2025 resolved the DMXL-WDR7-ROGDI complex. Function is established experimentally, though disease relevance (ALS, OCD associations) is only correlative.
