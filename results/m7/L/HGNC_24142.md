TM2D1 (also called beta-amyloid-binding protein, BBP) is a multi-pass membrane protein reported in 2001 to bind amyloid-beta and sensitise neurons to its toxicity, although a 2003 study showed that the proposed G-protein coupling is not direct. Fly work on the TM2D family indicates shared roles in Notch signalling, but the mammalian mechanism for TM2D1 remains unresolved. Other reports are expression or pharmacology associations in cancer and bioinformatic studies.
