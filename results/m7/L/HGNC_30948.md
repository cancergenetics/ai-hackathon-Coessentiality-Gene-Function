ZNF585B is a poorly characterised C2H2 Kruppel-type zinc finger protein on chromosome 19q13 that is annotated only by similarity as a possible transcriptional regulator. It appears in two high-throughput or bioinformatic lists (a CRISPR drug-resistance screen hit and a colon cancer prognostic gene signature) without any follow-up. No focused functional study of this gene was found.
