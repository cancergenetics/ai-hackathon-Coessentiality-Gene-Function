ATP5MC2 (formerly ATP5G2) is one of three genes encoding the identical mature c-subunit of the mitochondrial ATP synthase F0 domain, which forms the c-ring rotor driving proton translocation. A triple knockout of ATP5G1/2/3 shows the c-subunit is dispensable for assembly of a vestigial ATP synthase and for the permeability transition pore, and c-subunit methylation by FAM173B is needed for proper incorporation and ATP synthesis. Protein function is well established, whereas gene-specific (isoform-specific) work is limited to differences in mitochondrial targeting sequences.
