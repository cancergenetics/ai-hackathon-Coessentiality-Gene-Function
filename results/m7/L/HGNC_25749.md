TANGO6 is a membrane protein of the COPI vesicle system that carries RNA polymerase II subunit RPB2 from the cis-Golgi through the ER to the nucleus during G1, so loss of TANGO6 blocks RPB2 nuclear entry and impairs proliferation. A 2026 report presents it as a nucleocytoplasmic RNA-binding protein recruited with Pol II to promoters and also regulating translation, and zebrafish tango6 mutants fail in HSPC proliferation and definitive haematopoiesis. A CDH1-TANGO6 germline deletion in a gastric-cancer family and CRISPR-dependency prognostic signatures are only associative.
