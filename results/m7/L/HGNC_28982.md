Summary: HMGXB3 is a nuclear HMG-box-containing protein with no curated molecular function. Published work mainly treats HMGXB3 as a regulated target (miR-324-5p, miR-1224-3p, Roquin-mediated mRNA decay) linked to cell motility/cytoskeleton phenotypes, and a mouse transcriptomic study hints at DNA-damage/cell-cycle involvement. What the HMGXB3 protein itself does is unresolved.
