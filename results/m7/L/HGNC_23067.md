TRAPPC5 is a small core subunit of the TRAPP tethering complexes (the Trs31/BET3-family), which act as Rab1/Rab11 guanine nucleotide exchange factors in ER-to-Golgi trafficking. The retrieved literature gives only complex-level information plus one hepatocellular carcinoma paper reporting growth and invasion effects of TRAPPC5 manipulation. Gene-specific mechanistic or structural data on TRAPPC5 were not retrieved.
