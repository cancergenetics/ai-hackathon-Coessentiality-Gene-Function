CYRIA (CYRI-A, FAM49A) is a RAC1-binding protein of the CYRI family related to CYRI-B, a negative regulator of the Scar/WAVE complex through RAC1 sequestration. CYRI-A is transiently recruited to nascent macropinosomes in a PI3K- and RAC1-dependent manner and regulates macropinosome formation and integrin internalisation, thereby limiting invasive migration. The 2p24.2 locus is linked to orofacial clefting but CYRIA itself was not required for craniofacial development in chick embryos.
