TADA2B (hAda2beta) is a human homologue of the yeast SAGA adaptor Ada2 that associates with GCN5-containing TFTC/STAGA and with SWI/SNF and coactivates Pax5-dependent transcription together with GCN5 or BRG1. Recent work implicates Tada2b in H3K9ac/H2Bub regulation in hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells and in XRCC1-dependent DNA repair. Function as a SAGA histone-acetyltransferase module component is established by focused biochemical and functional studies.
