CHIC2 encodes a small palmitoylated membrane-associated protein localised to vesicles and the plasma membrane and was first cloned from the t(4;12) ETV6 translocation in acute myeloid leukaemia. Recent focused work shows that it acts as an adapter/inhibitory partner of the E3 ligase STUB1 (CHIP), controlling ubiquitination and stability of cytokine receptors such as CSF2RB and IL-27Ra in T cells. Much of the remaining literature is clinical (CHIC2 deletion as a FISH surrogate for FIP1L1-PDGFRA).
