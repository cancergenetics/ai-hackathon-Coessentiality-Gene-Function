COMMD3 is a COMM-domain protein that forms a heterodimer with COMMD2 within the CCC subcomplex of the Commander endosomal recycling machinery, with additional Commander-independent roles via ARF1 stabilisation. Focused studies link it to lysosomal function (GCase delivery, mannose-6-phosphate receptor trafficking), and a COMMD3/COMMD8 module acts as a signalling adaptor for chemoattractant receptors in B-cell migration. Earlier reports connected it to NF-kB and ENaC regulation.
