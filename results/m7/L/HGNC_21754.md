TMEM60 is an uncharacterised multi-pass membrane protein with no biochemical function reported. The only gene-specific experimental work is in glioma, where one study reports high expression, prognostic association and reduced proliferation after knockdown (a second, AKT-signalling paper was retracted). It otherwise appears in prognostic and biomarker gene signatures.
