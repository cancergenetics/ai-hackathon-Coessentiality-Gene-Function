WASHC3 (CCDC53) is a small core subunit of the pentameric WASH regulatory complex (with WASH, FAM21/WASHC2, SWIP/WASHC4 and strumpellin/WASHC5) that drives Arp2/3-dependent actin nucleation on endosomes for tubule fission and cargo sorting. Its assembly into the complex is promoted by the trimeric chaperone HSBP1, and in a WASH/WAVE assembly preprint it interacts with BRK1 and HSBP1. Human variants impair WASH-complex participation and PTH1R endosomal trafficking, causing a syndrome of short stature and variable neurodevelopmental abnormalities, and zebrafish/cardiomyocyte knockouts disrupt mitochondrial protein homeostasis.
