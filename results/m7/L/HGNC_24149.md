HES4 is a Hairy/Enhancer-of-split bHLH transcriptional repressor and a Notch target that, in human cancer cells, represses SLC44A2 and SDS to support mitochondrial redox balance and pyrimidine synthesis, and acts as a transcriptional activator at the COL4A2 promoter and in T-ALL survival signalling. Focused studies are recent and cancer-centred, with regulation by phosphorylation (via B4GALNT1/p38-JNK) and by EGFR-driven stabilisation. Its role in normal physiology is less defined.
