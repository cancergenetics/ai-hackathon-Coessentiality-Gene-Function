Summary: YIPF2 is a multi-pass Golgi membrane protein of the YIP1 family that localises to the medial/trans-Golgi and TGN and forms stable complexes with YIPF6 (and YIPF1). Its depletion delays Golgi reassembly and lowers glycan synthesis, and it promotes surface recycling of cargo such as CD147 and TNFRSF10B via Rab GTPases (Rab5/Rab22a, RAB8). A whole-genome RNAi screen also implicates it in genome stability and HR repair.
