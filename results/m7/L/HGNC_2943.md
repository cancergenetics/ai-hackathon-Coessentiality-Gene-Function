Summary: DNAH12 is an inner-dynein-arm axonemal dynein heavy chain, atypical in lacking a microtubule-binding domain, that is required in sperm flagella (not motile cilia) for recruiting DNAH1 and DNALI1 and for stabilising the central pair via radial spoke head proteins. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause asthenoteratozoospermia/MMAF and male infertility, replicated in Dnah12 knockout mice, with ICSI as an effective treatment. It is expressed from secondary spermatocytes to round spermatids.
