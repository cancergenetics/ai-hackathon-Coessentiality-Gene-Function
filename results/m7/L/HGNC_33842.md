AARD (formerly C8orf85) encodes an alanine- and arginine-rich domain-containing protein of unknown biochemical function. The only focused work is on the mouse ortholog, which is expressed specifically in Sertoli cells of the testis and is a direct transcriptional target of the androgen receptor. No human functional study, protein function or loss-of-function phenotype was found, so the class is partial (expression and regulation only).
