LEPROTL1 (endospanin-2) is a small multi-pass membrane protein of the OB-RGRP/VPS55 family. Mouse transgenic and silencing work shows it lowers hepatic growth-hormone receptor surface levels and GH signalling, and a 2026 study identifies LEPROT/LEPROTL1 as COPI-binding Golgi-retention receptors for transmembrane proteins such as EGFR and TFRC. Function is established by focused experimental studies, although the full mechanism linking the Golgi role to GH-receptor regulation is still being worked out.
