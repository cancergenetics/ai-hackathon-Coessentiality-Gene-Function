**Summary.** SLITRK2 encodes a single-pass type I postsynaptic adhesion molecule enriched in neurons that binds presynaptic PTPdelta and drives excitatory synapse differentiation. It interacts through a C-terminal PDZ-binding motif with PSD-95 family scaffolds and Shank3, and negatively regulates NTRK2. X-linked variants cause a neurodevelopmental disorder, so function is well established experimentally.
