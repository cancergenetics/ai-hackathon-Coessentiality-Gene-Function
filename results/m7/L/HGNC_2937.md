Summary: DMXL1 (Rabconnectin-3A) is the mammalian homologue of yeast Rav1 and, with WDR7 and ROGDI, forms the RAVE (mRAVE) complex that assembles the V1 and V0 domains of the V-ATPase on endolysosomal membranes to drive acidification. Knockdown, mouse genetics and viral antagonism studies show it is required for lysosomal acidification, autophagy and embryonic survival. Mouse alleles and patient variants link it to craniofacial development.
