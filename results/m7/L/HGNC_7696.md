NDUFB10 (PDSW) is an accessory subunit in the membrane arm (PD module) of mitochondrial complex I that is imported via the CHCHD4/MIA40 disulfide relay and is needed for assembly of the holoenzyme and respiratory supercomplexes. Biallelic NDUFB10 variants cause isolated complex I deficiency with lactic acidosis and cardiomyopathy, and knockout cells lose the P_D module. Function is established by focused genetic and biochemical studies.
