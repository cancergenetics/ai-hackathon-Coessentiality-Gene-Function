TM2D2 is a multi-pass membrane protein of the conserved TM2D family (with TM2D1 and TM2D3) and has no mammalian gene-specific functional study in the retrieved literature. Fly work on the whole family shows shared roles in Notch signalling at the gamma-secretase step, and a Daphnia CRISPR study implicates the TM2D2 orthologue in an inducible morphological defence. Direct evidence for human TM2D2 is lacking.
