**Summary.** TLNRD1 (formerly MESDC1) encodes a talin R7R8-like four-helix rod-domain protein that binds and bundles F-actin and promotes filopodia formation. More recent work shows it interacts with CCM2, is a component of the cerebral cavernous malformation complex, and maintains endothelial actin organisation and barrier integrity, linking it to coronary artery disease risk. Function is established by focused biochemical, structural and cell/zebrafish studies.
