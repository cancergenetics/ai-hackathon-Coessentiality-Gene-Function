PSMD13 (RPN9, p40.5) is a PCI-domain subunit of the lid of the 19S regulatory particle of the 26S proteasome, with the yeast Rpn9 ortholog shown to be required for efficient proteasome assembly, Rpn10 incorporation and cell-cycle progression. Human cryo-EM structures place it in the lid, and ortholog work in fish suggests an NF-kB regulatory role via TAK1 ubiquitination. Core function is established by focused yeast genetics and structural work; human gene-specific experimental work is more limited.
