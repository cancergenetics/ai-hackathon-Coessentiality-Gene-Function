CNOT10 is a vertebrate/eukaryote-conserved subunit of the CCR4-NOT mRNA deadenylase complex that, together with CNOT11, forms a module docked on the N-terminal region of the CNOT1 scaffold. Focused studies show that the CNOT10/CNOT11 module contributes to tubulin mRNA autoregulation and to TTP-mediated mRNA metabolism, while genome-wide screens connect it to dsRNA sensing and interferon-responsive microglia. Its precise biochemical contribution within the complex is still being resolved.
