BICRAL (GLTSCR1L) is a subunit of the non-canonical/GBAF SWI/SNF chromatin remodelling complex, where it is mutually exclusive with its paralog BICRA together with BRD9. Focused biochemistry defined this membership and showed cell-type-specific proliferation dependence on GLTSCR1/GLTSCR1L knockouts, but a recent structural study finds BICRAL largely dispensable for complex assembly and remodelling activity. Its specific function therefore remains only partly defined.
