Summary: KDF1 (keratinocyte differentiation factor 1) is a cytoplasmic/cell-junction protein that restrains basal epidermal progenitor proliferation and promotes differentiation, partly by binding IKKa and USP7 to stabilise IKKa, and by inhibiting NF-kB signalling in ameloblasts. Mouse genetics and human variants (ectodermal dysplasia 12, tooth agenesis, enamel and hidradenitis phenotypes) establish its role in ectodermal development. Cancer papers report context-dependent oncogenic or tumour-suppressive roles.
