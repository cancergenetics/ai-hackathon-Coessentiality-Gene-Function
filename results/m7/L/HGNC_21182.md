NUP43 is a WD40 beta-propeller subunit of the Nup107-160 (Y-complex) scaffold of the nuclear pore, which also localises to kinetochores in mitosis. Focused evidence on the individual protein is mostly from a Drosophila null mutant showing fertility and early-embryo defects, from a human link to premature ovarian insufficiency, and from a CRISPR-based study linking NUP43 to PD-L1 nuclear import in colorectal cancer. Complex-level kinetochore and NPC-assembly functions are established but rarely dissected for NUP43 alone.
