TMEM209 (NET31) is a two-transmembrane-domain protein of the nuclear envelope that is now classified as a transmembrane nucleoporin: it sits in the nuclear pore complex and binds Nup210, and it also interacts with NUP205 to stabilise it and sustain nuclear c-Myc. Depletion impairs cell-cycle entry, and the C. elegans orthologue NPP-28 positions PLK-1 at the nuclear envelope during mitosis, while cancer studies link it to lung and liver tumour growth through NUP205 and KPNB1.
