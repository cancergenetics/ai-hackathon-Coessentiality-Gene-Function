G2E3 is a G2/M-expressed ubiquitin ligase with a catalytically inactive HECT domain and two PHD/RING-like ubiquitin-ligase domains, essential for early mouse embryogenesis and shuttling between nucleus, nucleolus and cytoplasm. A 2025 Nature study established it as the H3K14-monoubiquitin ligase that recruits SUV39H and HP1 to build pericentromeric heterochromatin. Additional recent work links it to autophagosome-lysosome fusion via GABARAP binding and to substrate ubiquitination in cancer.
