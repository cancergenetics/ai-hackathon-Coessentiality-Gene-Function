**Summary.** GRIPAP1 (GRASP-1) is a neuron-enriched coiled-coil protein associated with the AMPA receptor scaffold GRIP1 that acts as an effector of Rab4 and a link to Rab11 domains to coordinate recycling endosome maturation in dendrites. It is required for activity-dependent AMPA receptor recycling, spine morphology and synaptic plasticity, and a cleaved C-terminal fragment scaffolds MEKK1-JNK1 signalling. Genomically it is also a TFE3 fusion partner in some renal carcinomas.
