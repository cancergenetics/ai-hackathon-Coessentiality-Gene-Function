PGBD2 is one of five human genes derived from domesticated piggyBac transposases and has no gene-specific functional study in the retrieved literature. A 2022 family-level study found that none of the human PGBDs transposes piggyBac-like elements and that their expression varies with cell type and stress, hinting at domesticated but unknown functions. Otherwise PGBD2 appears only in expression or biomarker gene lists.
