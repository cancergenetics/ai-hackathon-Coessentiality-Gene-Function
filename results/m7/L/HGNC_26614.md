Summary: ANKRD52 is an ankyrin-repeat regulatory subunit of protein phosphatase 6 (PP6) that associates with the PPP6C catalytic subunit and SAPS-domain subunits. Focused studies show the ANKRD52-PPP6C complex dephosphorylates AGO2 to sustain miRNA-mediated silencing, and ANKRD52 loss/mutation in tumours affects miR-155/SOCS1-dependent interferon signalling. Further work links it to TAZ/PAK1 regulation in lung adenocarcinoma.
