LGALS9B encodes a galectin-9 paralog annotated only as a galactoside-binding protein. A single focused 2025 study in gastric cancer reports that it competes with the E3 ligase HERC5 for binding EEF1D, stabilising EEF1D and activating PI3K/AKT signalling to promote proliferation, migration and metastasis. Other mentions are network or methylation-signature hits in cancer datasets.
