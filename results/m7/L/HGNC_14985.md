**Summary.** LUZP1 is a leucine-zipper protein that cross-links actin filaments, localises to centrosome/basal body and stress fibres, and associates with microtubules. It restricts primary cilia formation together with EPLIN, regulates myosin light chain phosphorylation during apical constriction and cytokinesis, and stabilises CERF chromatin remodeling complexes in embryonic stem cells. Mouse knockouts show neural tube and cardiovascular defects.
