COMMD2 is one of ten COMM-domain proteins that form the hetero-decameric ring of the CCC subcomplex of the 16-subunit Commander complex (with CCDC22/CCDC93, DENND10 and Retriever), which mediates endosomal recycling of transmembrane cargo. Cryo-EM and crystallographic studies establish its structural position but not a gene-specific mechanism. A focused 2022-2026 study shows that pneumococcal infection triggers COMMD2 binding to NF-kB p65 and p62, promoting p65 export and degradation and altering host immunity.
