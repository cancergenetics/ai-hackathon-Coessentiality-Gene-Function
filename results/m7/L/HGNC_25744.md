AKIRIN1 is a conserved nuclear adapter protein, downstream of myostatin, that promotes myoblast differentiation and is suppressed by glucocorticoid-induced myostatin. Akirin1 knockout mice show a shift in muscle fibre type (more IIa, fewer type I fibres) and Akirin1 regulates MuRF-1 promoter activity in reporter assays. Additional reports link Akirin1 to cancer-associated fibroblast activation in lung cancer and to ferroptosis via the EGR1/TP53 axis in kidney ischaemia-reperfusion injury, but its biochemical mechanism remains uncertain.
