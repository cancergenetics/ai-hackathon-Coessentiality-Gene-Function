NDUFA2 (complex I subunit B8) is a supernumerary accessory subunit of mitochondrial respiratory complex I that is not part of the catalytic core. Patient mutations cause complex I deficiency with Leigh syndrome or cystic leukoencephalopathy, with rescue of complex I by NDUFA2 re-expression, and knockout/tagging studies show that it is required for assembly of a functional complex. Function is established by focused genetic and biochemical studies.
