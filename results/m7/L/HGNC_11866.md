**Summary.** TMEFF1 (tomoregulin-1) is a single-pass type I membrane protein with EGF-like and follistatin-like domains, expressed predominantly in brain neurons. Early Xenopus and cell-culture work showed it binds the nodal co-receptor Cripto and dampens nodal/BMP signalling, and in 2024 it was shown to be a neuron-specific restriction factor that blocks HSV-1 entry by binding NECTIN-1, with human loss-of-function variants linked to herpes simplex encephalitis. Its function is therefore established by focused experimental work.
