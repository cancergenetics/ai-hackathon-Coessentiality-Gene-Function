PSG2 (pregnancy-specific beta-1-glycoprotein 2) is a secreted placental protein of the CEA/immunoglobulin-superfamily PSG cluster on chromosome 19q13, expressed by syncytiotrophoblast. The only focused experimental study (2026) shows that PSG2 activates TGF-beta/Smad3 signalling in trophoblast cells and restrains trophoblast EMT, migration and invasion, being elevated in preeclampsia; otherwise it appears in biomarker/proteomic lists and regulatory studies (CTCF, KLF10). Its normal biochemical function is only partially characterised, so the class is partial.
