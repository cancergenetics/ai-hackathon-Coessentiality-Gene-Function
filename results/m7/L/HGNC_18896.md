**Summary.** TMEM185B (formerly FAM11B) is an uncharacterised predicted multipass membrane protein encoded on chromosome 2; its former name FAM11B relates it to FAM11A (TMEM185A). The only specific early report concerns its discovery as a transcribed copy of FAM11A, and later papers merely list it among differentially expressed genes. No experimental study of its function, localisation or interactions was retrieved.
