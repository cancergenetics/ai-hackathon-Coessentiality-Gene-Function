**Summary.** UNC50 is a Golgi-localised multipass membrane protein whose C. elegans ortholog keeps muscle nicotinic acetylcholine receptors from lysosomal degradation. In human cells UNC50 supports early endosome-to-Golgi trafficking of Shiga toxin 2 by recruiting the ARF-GEF GBF1, and a biallelic loss-of-function variant was reported in lethal arthrogryposis multiplex congenita. An early (2000) report described the mammalian homologue UNCL as an inner nuclear membrane RNA-binding protein.
