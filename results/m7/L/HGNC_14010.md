**Summary.** MGA is a large nuclear protein combining a bHLHZip domain that heterodimerises with MAX and a T-box DNA-binding domain, acting as a dual-specificity transcriptional repressor/activator. It is a defining DNA-binding subunit of the non-canonical Polycomb complex PRC1.6 together with MAX, E2F6, L3MBTL2 and PCGF6, and antagonises MYC/E2F target expression. Inactivating mutations in lung adenocarcinoma and leukaemias, plus in vivo mouse work, establish a tumour-suppressor role.
