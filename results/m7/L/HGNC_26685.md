Summary: CCDC71L (C7orf74) is an uncharacterised coiled-coil protein with no UniProt functional annotation. Indirect evidence comes from a pig orthologue (FLJ36031) acting as a positive regulator of intramuscular fat deposition and from a TNBC ceRNA study where CCDC71L supports proliferation, plus GWAS/prognostic associations. No biochemical mechanism, partners or localisation are known.
