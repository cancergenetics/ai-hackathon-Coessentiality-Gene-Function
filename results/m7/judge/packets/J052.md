**Summary.** GENE_X is a p24-family cargo receptor of the early secretory pathway (ER, ERGIC, cis-Golgi) that oligomerises with TMED2/9/10 and is required for ER-to-Golgi-to-plasma-membrane export of Toll-like receptors, notably TLR4. It also acts as a negative regulator of TRAM-dependent TLR4 signalling from endosomes and is itself degraded by the rhomboid protease RHBDL4. Focused functional work is largely confined to TLR trafficking and innate immune signalling, with a recent preprint implicating a GENE_X/2/9/10 entrapment complex in proteinopathy cargo retention.
