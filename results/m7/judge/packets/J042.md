**Summary.** Summary: GENE_X is a subunit of the CCC (COMMD/CCDC22/CCDC93) complex that, with Retriever, forms the Commander complex driving endosomal recycling of transmembrane cargo, within which it forms a heterodimer with COMMD4. In lymphocytes the COMMD3/8 pair acts as an adaptor that recruits GRK6 to chemoattractant receptors such as CXCR4, and GENE_X also contributes to NF-kB/IkB regulation with CCDC22. Many cancer papers report GENE_X as a ceRNA-axis target, but these add little mechanism.
