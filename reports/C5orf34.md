## 0. Bench summary (for a bench biologist; plain words)

- **Claim:** C5orf34 — an uncharacterised 638-aa protein with no recognised domain beyond its own family — acts in **centriole duplication**. Its co-essentiality partners are, without exception, centriole proteins: SPICE1, CEP135, CEP295, WRAP73, PPP1R35, CEP120, CCP110, CCSAP, plus CCDC77, MIIP and TBC1D31/WDR67, which were shown in 2025 to form the **A–C linker** at the centriole's proximal end [F7]. The relationship type assigned by the frozen rule is **b** (assembly or maturation factor), i.e. the claim reads "required for assembly of the centriole", not "is a centriolar subunit" — no physical-interaction evidence supports a subunit claim.
- **Confidence:** p_cal = **0.0443** (95% interval 0.020–0.090). This is the expected probability that **the predicted term** GO:1902115 "regulation of organelle assembly" is confirmed by experimental (D1-evidence) GO annotation within about eight years. It is *not* the probability that the biology above is right, and it does *not* cover the relationship type. Tier: none (p_cal fill); rank 31 of 40.
- **Strongest reason to doubt:** apart from the DepMap profile there is **no orthogonal support at all** — the physical class was measured and gives no support (one edge vs 0.43 expected), co-abundance, localisation and structure were not measured for this protein, and the perturbation class gives no support; the prediction fails both top-40 checks (DEMETER2 non-replication, TP53 flag); and the co-essentiality signal is carried by neighbourhood overlap (z_B 23.8) rather than by direct profile correlation (z_F 2.4; raw Chronos r with SPICE1 = 0.06 †).
- **First experiment:** knock a fluorescent tag into the endogenous C5orf34 locus in a dependent line and ask, by expansion microscopy, whether the protein sits on the centriole (and where), while scoring centriole number after acute depletion beside SPICE1 depletion; time ≈ 12–16 weeks; approximate cost €30–40k (details in Appendix C).
- Cancer-line essentiality is not organismal function: dependency in DepMap lines says the gene is needed for proliferation there, not what it does in the organism.

**Honest reading of the two failures.**

*TP53.* C5orf34 knockout is **less** damaging in TP53-mutant lines (+0.035). Recomputed genome-wide †, the median of this difference over 18,434 genes is −0.0007 and there is no per-line screen-level shift (median of the per-line mean effect: −0.149 in mutant vs −0.1486 in wild-type lines), so the difference is real and sits at the 94.8th percentile — but it is small in absolute terms. Two biological readings are available and neither can be settled here. (i) C5orf34 is a direct target of the **p53–DREAM** repressor: it appears in the published list of 268 genes repressed through the p53–LIN37/DREAM pathway [F6], so its expression — and therefore any dependency on it — is itself p53-dependent. (ii) Loss of centrioles triggers a **USP28–53BP1–p53–p21** arrest [F10], so any true centriole-duplication gene is expected to be *less* essential where p53 is broken. Against both readings: the flag is **not shared with the partners** — recomputed †, SPICE1 gives +0.000 (p 0.83), WRAP73 −0.005, CEP135 +0.019, CEP44 +0.022, PCM1 −0.026; only CEP295 (+0.044) resembles C5orf34. If C5orf34 were simply a centriole-duplication gene, one would expect SPICE1 and CEP135 to show the same p53 dependence; they do not. This is the single most uncomfortable observation in the report and is left unresolved.

*DEMETER2.* Mean partner r 0.055 vs null 0.073. Context: the check fails for 182 of 204 measured genes in the evaluated pool (`results/ledger/M6_SUMMARY.md`), so it has little discriminating power; and for this gene it was never likely to pass, because the CRISPR signal itself is not a direct profile correlation (z_F 2.4; raw Chronos r with SPICE1 0.06 †) and RNAi knockdown is partial. Recomputed over all DEMETER2 lines †, the only partners with any positive signal are CEP295 (r = 0.196, n = 285) and CEP135 (r = 0.136, n = 343), while TBC1D31 (−0.146) and CCSAP (−0.128) run the other way. Reported as a failure.

## 7. Literature (blinded step, then synthesis)

**Blinded evidence (agent L, `results/m7/L/HGNC_24738.md`, 6 items, N2).** Two functional cancer studies — siRNA knockdown suppresses lung-adenocarcinoma proliferation and motility with reduced p-MEK/p-ERK [F1], and knockdown/overexpression plus RNA-seq implicate cell-cycle modulation in LUAD [F2] — plus a pan-cancer bioinformatic study [F3] and three mentions (endometrial-carcinoma ceRNA analysis, a prognostic signature, and differential CpG methylation in gestational diabetes [F4]). None DepMap-derived. L's summary: "No biochemical function, interaction partner or localisation has been established, so the evidence is phenotypic and indirect."

**Blinded verdicts (`results/m7/judge/`, packet J065).** Real statement ("functions in regulation of organelle assembly"): **0 (unaddressed)** by both judges — verbatim justification "Rows 1-2 report cell cycle/MAPK roles in cancer, not organelle assembly." Decoys (alcohol metabolic process; histone modifying activity; vesicle organization): 0, 0, 0. Judges J1 and J2 agreed; adjudicator J3 n/a (overall κ = 0.975 over 360 statements). d = 0.0; the gene contributes nothing to the primary sign-flip result.

**Unblinded synthesis.** Three independent lines, none of them a direct test, now point the same way.

1. *Cell-cycle expression and p53–DREAM control.* C5orf34 is one of 701 genes whose expression tracks S/G2-M across human and mouse transcriptomes, and it is listed among the 205 putative cell-cycle genes that are "functionally uncharacterized" [F5]. In the same paper, 27 such orphans were screened for localisation and three (C3orf14, CCDC150, C18orf54) were found on centrosomes — C5orf34 was *not* among the proteins shown to be centrosomal, so this paper neither supports nor refutes the localisation. Independently, C5orf34 appears in the published list of genes repressed through the p53–LIN37/DREAM pathway [F6]. A gene that is expressed in S/G2-M and switched off by p53-DREAM is exactly what a centriole-duplication factor looks like transcriptionally — and also what many other cell-cycle genes look like.
2. *The partners are a bona fide, recently solved module.* In 2025, CCDC77, WDR67 (= TBC1D31) and MIIP — ranks 3, 4 and 2 in C5orf34's neighbourhood — were shown by ultrastructure expansion microscopy to localise between microtubule triplets at the **A–C linker** and to form a complex whose depletion breaks the centriole's proximal end and impairs duplication [F7]. That paper found MIIP in the first place by the same kind of analysis used here: "we conducted a cross-DepMap analysis … Among the top 100 genes, we identified 12 that shared co-dependencies with CCDC77 and WDR67: WDR8, SPICE1, CEP135, TUBE1, TUBD1, TEDC2, RTTN (rotatin), CEP44, CEP295, PPP1R35, CEP152, and MIIP" [F7]. Eight of those twelve are in C5orf34's K = 25 neighbourhood. Note carefully: because that discovery is itself DepMap-derived, under DESIGN §6.4 it counts as **unaddressed**, not as support, for the blinded judging — and the same caution applies to reading it as independent confirmation here. What it does establish is that this particular co-dependency neighbourhood has already yielded one correct, experimentally validated centriolar protein.
3. *SPICE1 and CEP135 define the step.* SPICE1 localises to centrioles throughout the cell cycle and its depletion "impairs centriole duplication and causes severe mitotic defects" [F8]; CEP120 and SPICE1 are required to localise CEP135 to procentrioles and control centriole length with CPAP [F9]. So the module C5orf34 is attached to is procentriole assembly/elongation, which is what the experiment in Appendix C tests.

Against all of this stands §6: no physical, co-abundance, localisation or structural evidence for C5orf34 itself, and three independent interaction resources that place it elsewhere.

**Closest prior publication and what co-essentiality adds.** The closest prior statements are that C5orf34 is a cell-cycle-regulated, p53-DREAM-repressed, functionally uncharacterised gene [F5, F6] whose knockdown slows cancer-cell proliferation [F1, F2]. **No publication placing C5orf34 at the centriole, or assigning it any molecular function, was found in the literature as of 2026-10-30.** Co-essentiality adds a specific, testable module assignment — centriole duplication, alongside the A–C linker proteins — and a specific experiment (Appendix C) that would decide it in one pass.

**Prior prediction lists containing this gene:** Wainberg 2021 — not found in the retrieved full text (supplementary tables not searched) [F11]; hu.MAP 3.0 — present, in four non-centriolar complexes (§6); Replogle 2022 — perturbed in the K562 genome-wide library (class G, no support) and not among the 1,974 rows of `annotated_embedding_coordinates.csv`; Predictomes — present, no centriolar pair (§6).

**Post-freeze (after 2026-10-29) papers confirming the prediction:** none found as of 2026-10-30. [F7] was published 2025-07-24, before the freeze.

## 8. Alternative explanations

- *Proximity / same-locus / copy number.* No partner on chromosome 5; 0 of 8 supporting partners carry a locus or CN flag (|r| ≤ 0.05).
- *Hub attraction.* Hub share 0.00; 0 hub partners.
- *Lineage.* Removing Eye leaves the top term unchanged, though N(g) overlap is 0.80 — one partner in five changes, the weakest neighbourhood stability of the genes reported in this pass. No TF enrichment; no dependency–expression coupling.
- *p53 confound.* Discussed in §5; two biological readings [F6, F10], neither confirmed, and the flag is not shared with the centriolar partners. Mitigated in the experiment by using TP53-mutant lines (SNU-1750, HAP1) and by scoring centriole number directly rather than growth.
- *RNAi non-replication.* Discussed in §5.
- *Weak-dependency statistics.* Only 18 of 1,361 lines reach Chronos ≤ −0.5 and the mean effect is −0.070; much of the neighbourhood evidence therefore rests on small effects measured well. The split-half check (R = 0.424 vs null max 0.184, p = 0.0050) says the neighbourhood is reproducible, not that the effect is large.
- *Calibration family.* p_cal was fitted in the "other" family because `centrosome_cilia_mitosis` has fewer than 30 H genes (`results/ledger/M6_SUMMARY.md`); the calibration therefore does not use any information about how well this method performs on centrosome modules. p_cal = 0.0443 should be read with that caveat.

**Strongest competing biological hypothesis.** That C5orf34 is a **cell-cycle-coexpressed gene whose dependency tracks proliferation and p53-DREAM status rather than centriole biology** — i.e. relationship type **e** in disguise. Its expression is S/G2-M-restricted [F5] and p53-DREAM-repressed [F6]; the DepMap profile of such genes can co-vary with other S/G2-M genes (which the centriole genes are) without any physical or pathway relationship. The discriminating observations are localisation and the centriole-count phenotype: a type-e gene has no reason to sit on the centriole or to produce a specific duplication failure. Appendix C.6 states the experiment.

A second competing hypothesis is **ciliogenesis rather than centriole duplication** (TBC1D31, CEP41, WRAP73 and PCM1 all carry cilium annotations). This is addressed by including a ciliation assay in the Tier-1 arm.

## 9. Falsifying observations (written from the prediction; see the process note above)

1. Endogenously tagged C5orf34 shows no centriolar or centriolar-satellite signal in two dependent lines by expansion microscopy, while the same knock-in is detected elsewhere in the cell — the module call would then rest on co-essentiality alone and should be downgraded to type e.
2. Acute depletion of C5orf34, titrated to the same growth cost as SPICE1 depletion, leaves centriole number, centriole length and the proximal-end markers (CEP135, CEP44, CEP295) unchanged in both dependent lines.
3. C5orf34 depletion is fully additive with SPICE1 or CEP135 depletion in centriole number and in growth (no epistasis), and tagged C5orf34 pulls down none of SPICE1, CEP135, CCDC77, MIIP or TBC1D31.

### C.6 Experiment that separates the alternative explanation

- **Alternative:** C5orf34's dependency reflects S/G2-M co-expression and p53-DREAM control (type e) rather than centriole function.

### C.7 Decision rule (fixed in advance)

- **Supported:** dropout rescued by guide-resistant cDNA **AND** centriole-number or proximal-architecture defect beyond the PPME1 control at matched growth effect **AND** centriolar (or centriolar-satellite) localisation of the endogenous tag **AND** Tier-2 physical or epistatic support (IP-MS enrichment of the centriolar partners at SAINT BFDR ≤ 0.01, or epistasis with SPICE1/CCDC77).
- **Refuted:** dropout not rescued, or localisation incompatible with the centriole/satellite compartment in both dependent lines.
- **Ambiguous:** anything else — reported as ambiguous with a proposed alternative relationship type (e, if the phenotype tracks proliferation and p53 status only; or a cilium-branch reassignment).
